# Claw’d Companion 0.6.0

## Desktop companions

- Claw’d has a native Mac app and a desktop companion for Windows and Linux. The repository documents platform-specific capabilities, including window-manager support.
- The idle arcade sends two randomized waves of pixel viruses and a boss onto the desktop. Enemies have individual health bars. Collect health, shields and rapid fire, and listen for the change to the boss theme.
- Claw’d starts with 100 health. A defeat leaves a **GIT** gravestone for five minutes. Playing during active computer use is an explicit preference.
- The Highscores tab keeps personal arena statistics. Register an Arena account with a username, email address and password to play server-scored rounds when the Zumbyte service is available. The server records effective player input, kills, deaths, K/D, wins and score. Fully autonomous rounds do not enter the rankings.

## Updates and languages

- Check for releases directly from GitHub’s download URLs. The app displays the available version, Markdown release notes and a link to the appropriate package.
- The interface and documentation cover English, simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Bengali, Portuguese, Indonesian and Urdu, plus German.
- Open the information panel for version details and Zumbyte contact information.
