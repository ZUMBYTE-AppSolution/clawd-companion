# Changelog

## 0.7.3

Signed Mac builds download updates in the app, verify the publisher and open macOS Installer. Install the signed PKG manually once when upgrading from an earlier ad-hoc build.

## 0.7.2

Claw’d now prefers a free title bar for idle breaks lasting 45 to 100 seconds. Move the window and Claw’d moves with it. On Wayland, the companion window has its own pixel ledge.

## 0.7.1

Choose Light, Dark or Follow system under **Overlay → Appearance**. Changes take effect immediately and persist after restart. On Linux, the Wayland companion window, tabs, menus and form fields now use matching colors.

## 0.7.0

Open the maximized arena from High scores. Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Claw’d fires automatically. Each level has four waves and a boss. Start with three lives and earn one more every two completed levels. Combine piercing shots, multishot and blast damage. Your best level and wave are saved on this device.


## [0.6.1](docs/releases/0.6.1.md)

Play a local round directly from Highscores on Mac, Windows and Linux. The Mac companion can also display the account limits shown in Claude Desktop’s Usage settings.

## [0.6.0](docs/releases/0.6.0.md)

Cross-platform desktop companions, personal and ranked arena statistics, built-in Arena account registration and sign-in, a Zumbyte highscore service, GitHub update notifications, Markdown release notes, installation packages and a multilingual illustrated guide.

## 0.5.0

Added the idle-only virus arcade, enemy health bars, collectible power-ups, boss music, a persistent five-minute GIT grave and a resurrection animation.

## 0.4.0

Added the approval sign, window-title-bar interactions, Claude activity props, and cursor-tracking letter volleys.
